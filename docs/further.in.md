# Further measurements

Every measurement the paper has no room for, each in a sentence. [check.py](../check.py) recomputes every number here, [results.md](results.md) holds the per-trace and per-pair tables, and [method.md](method.md) says how each was made. AUROCs are medians over traces of the worst of four later windows, and a guess is wrong by the read's own latency unless period labels are named.

## Finding the wrong guesses

- **The traces.** Sixty cuts from 39 volumes qualify. Two are from a volume the table's design had seen and one has no read in its first minute, which leaves the {{HeldTraces}} from {{HeldVolumes}} volumes.
- **Both distances as Gohil et al. use them.** Flagging every input beyond the 90th percentile of the training distances, Euclidean and Mahalanobis distance flag a median {{EuclidFlags}} and {{MahalFlags}}% of reads. Trace by trace, a guess the Euclidean one flags is wrong more often than a guess it passes on {{EuclidFlaggedMore}} of the {{HeldTraces}} traces, by a median {{EuclidFlagGap}} points. For Mahalanobis, on {{MahalFlaggedMore}}, by {{MahalFlagGap}} points. As separate medians over traces, a flagged guess is wrong {{EuclidFlaggedWrong}}% of the time and a passed one {{EuclidPassedWrong}}% for Euclidean, {{MahalFlaggedWrong}} and {{MahalPassedWrong}}% for Mahalanobis.
- **One running count.** It is ahead of each of the nine estimators on {{OneCellAheadLo}} to {{OneCellAheadHi}} of the {{HeldTraces}} traces. With the guess held fixed it is a coin ({{AdmOneCell}} among reads admitted as fast).
- **With the guess held fixed.** Among reads Heimdall admits as fast, the table is ahead of each estimator on {{AdmAheadLo}} to {{AdmAheadHi}} traces and ahead of the best of them picked in hindsight on {{AdmAheadBest}}. Among reads Heimdall would move, asking which would have been fast, it scores {{MovTable}} against {{MovEstLo}} to {{MovEstHi}} and is ahead of the hindsight best on {{MovAheadBest}} of {{MovTraces}}. With the read's size fixed as well, it is no better among admitted reads than the network's own margin ({{SameAdmTable}} and {{SameAdmMargin}}).
- **A fitted reference.** A classifier fitted on every labelled read of the first minute reaches {{AdmSup}} among admitted reads: on these traces a read's fate is partly unwritten when it is submitted.
- **What distance does see.** A faint forecast of slowness. On the artefact's traces it leans the right way where Heimdall says fast ({{DevAdmEuclid}}) and the wrong way where Heimdall says slow ({{DevMovEuclid}} and {{DevMovMahal}}). Held out, even that fades ({{AdmEuclid}} and {{AdmMahal}} among admitted reads, {{MovEuclid}} and {{MovMahal}} among moved ones).
- **Period labels.** Judged by them the table is behind the best estimator on {{PerBehind}} of {{HeldTraces}} traces. The network's own margin scores {{PerMargin}} and the best of the nine {{PerEstHi}}.
- **Where the table was shaped.** On the artefact's twelve traces it scores {{DevTableLo}} to {{DevTableHi}} and the best of the nine {{DevBestLo}} to {{DevBestHi}}. With the guess held fixed it leads the hindsight best on all {{DevAdmAhead}}, both ways ({{DevAdmTable}} and {{DevMovTable}} against {{DevAdmBest}} and {{DevMovBest}}).
- **Where it loses.** All {{TableBehind}} held-out traces on which the best of the nine beats the table are UMass Financial volumes. Its threshold can also go stale: on one development replay whose latency drifts upward it scores {{DevWorstRaw}}.
- **How often Heimdall is wrong.** On {{DevWrongLo}} to {{DevWrongHi}}% of later reads on the artefact's traces and {{HeldWrongLo}} to {{HeldWrongHi}}% held out, by each read's own latency. By its own period labels, {{DevWrongPerLo}} to {{DevWrongPerHi}}% and {{HeldWrongPerLo}} to {{HeldWrongPerHi}}%. The artefact's own test filters {{FilteredLo}} to {{FilteredHi}}% of a trace's rows as noise before it splits them, and Heimdall's long-term evaluation reports {{HeimAccLo}} to {{HeimAccHi}}% accuracy after a single training session.
- **Two other decisions.** With controllers of our own trained on each request's outcome, the table scores {{RpcTableLo}} to {{RpcTableHi}} on RPC hedging against {{RpcMarginLo}} to {{RpcMarginHi}} for the network's own margin, and {{StoTableLo}} to {{StoTableHi}} on storage-server hedging against {{StoMarginLo}} to {{StoMarginHi}}. Distance trails there too ({{OtherDistLo}} to {{OtherDistHi}}).

## The table as a forecast

- **Against a retrained network.** The same network trained on each read's own latency class scores {{DirNetMed}}: the table is ahead of it on {{DirNetAhead}} of {{HeldTraces}} traces, by a median {{DirNetLead}}. With the drive's live state among its inputs the network scores {{DirLiveMed}} and is ahead of the table on {{DirLiveBehind}}.
- **On the artefact's twelve traces.** The table scores {{DirDevTableMed}} and Heimdall's output {{DirDevHeimMed}}.
- **As a call.** Calling slow when q is at least 1/2, the table is wrong on {{WrongTable}}% of held-out reads, Heimdall on {{WrongHeim}}%, the retrained network on {{WrongNet}}% and the one with live state on {{WrongLive}}% (medians over traces). Never calling slow is wrong on {{NeverSlowWrong}}%, so held out the table's call earns next to nothing over that, and Heimdall is wrong more often than never calling slow on {{HeimWorseThanNever}} of the {{HeldTraces}} traces. On the artefact's twelve traces the table is wrong on {{DevWrongTable}}% of reads, never calling slow on {{DevNeverSlowWrong}}% and Heimdall on {{DevWrongHeim}}%.

## The tail

- **Two identical drives.** Nine pairs and sixteen policies in {{IdRounds}} rounds, {{IdKept}} kept runs, {{IdShared}} of them while other users' jobs ran on other cores of the machine. One run is missing: P1's run with no admission control failed the criterion on all four attempts in its fifth round, so P1's ratios to that run rest on four rounds and its comparisons between policies on five. On the six artefact pairs the better distance with hedging ends at {{IdDistOverRuleLo}} to {{IdDistOverRuleHi}} times the table's p99.9 with hedging, and the oracle at {{IdOracleOverGateLo}} to {{IdOracleOverGateHi}} times the best of the three. Without hedging the better distance cuts Heimdall's p99.9 by a median {{IdCutLo}} to {{IdCutHi}}%. Ratios to the run with no admission control are in [results.md](results.md) with their spread over rounds. One pair's run with no admission control differs widely between rounds, so its ratios move with the round, while the comparisons between policies are taken within a round.
- **Routing on the table alone.** A read goes to both drives when q is at least 1/2, with no network and no hedging. On the six artefact pairs this has the lower p99.9 than Heimdall and copies {{IdTableOnlyMovedLo}} to {{IdTableOnlyMovedHi}}% of the reads where Heimdall moves {{IdHeimMovedLo}} to {{IdHeimMovedHi}}%. Heimdall keeps the lower mean on {{IdHeimMeanBelow}} of the six, and the lower p99.9 on the three short pairs.
- **The three short pairs.** On K, with hedging, Euclidean distance ends at {{IdKEuclid}} of no admission control and the table at {{IdKTable}}. On S1 and S3, whose p99.9 rests on {{IdSoneTail}} and {{IdSthreeTail}} reads, Heimdall with hedging, checked by a distance, by the table or by nothing, ends at {{IdSGateLo}} to {{IdSGateHi}} and hedging alone at {{IdSoneAlone}} and {{IdSthreeAlone}}.
- **Two unlike drives.** On an AHCI M.2 drive paired with a SATA drive that the busier client overloads, three pairs and five quiet rounds: without hedging, Heimdall alone, with either distance or with the table leaves the p99.9 of P2 and P5 at {{QuietNoLo}} to {{QuietNoHi}} of no admission control. With hedging every policy on all three pairs reaches {{QuietWithLo}} to {{QuietWithHi}}, and hedging alone {{QuietAloneLo}} to {{QuietAloneHi}}.
- **The floor of hedging.** A read that hedging rescues returns no sooner than the timeout plus one read on the other drive. Against the SATA drive's {{QuietTailMs}} ms tail that floor is {{FloorQuietLo}} to {{FloorQuietHi}} of the p99.9. On the identical drives, whose tails end at {{IdTailLoMs}} to {{IdTailHiMs}} ms, it is {{FloorIdLo}} to {{FloorIdHi}}. It holds on the long pairs and is no bound on S1 and S3.
- **What Gohil et al. report.** Their distance estimators cut Heimdall's p99.9 by {{StudyCutLo}} and {{StudyCutHi}}%, flagging {{StudyFlagLo}} and {{StudyFlagHi}}% of requests. Rebuilt from their description, the same estimators cut it by a median {{IdCutLo}} to {{IdCutHi}}% on the identical drives, by {{QuietCutLo}} to {{QuietCutHi}}% on the unlike pair, and by {{SimCutLo}} to {{SimCutHi}}% in a simulator. Read off their Figure 10, as an estimate, hedging alone already brings p99.9 from about {{FigHeimMs}} ms under Heimdall to about {{FigHedgeMs}} ms, the estimators reach about {{FigGateMs}} ms, and at p99.99 hedging and the estimators meet.
- **Heimdall's own logs.** The artefact's hedging baseline ends at {{ArtHedgeLo}} to {{ArtHedgeHi}} of no admission control and Heimdall at {{ArtHeimLo}} to {{ArtHeimHi}}. Heimdall's mean is {{ArtHeimMeanLo}} to {{ArtHeimMeanHi}} and hedging's {{ArtHedgeMeanLo}} to {{ArtHedgeMeanHi}}.
- **Two consumer NVMe drives of a busy machine and the simulator.** One run a pair with no record of what else the machine was doing, and a simulator that reproduces Heimdall's own replay poorly on two pairs: both are in [results.md](results.md) and nothing above rests on either alone.
